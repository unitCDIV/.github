# Code of Conduct

This code applies to every Unit 404 (CDIV) space: repositories in the `unitCDIV` organisation, issues, pull requests and discussions, cohort sessions and chats, mentoring conversations, and events where you represent CDIV. It applies to learners, mentors, maintainers and visitors alike.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone here is learning something.
- Critique work, not people. Make review comments specific, kind and actionable.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Credit other people's work, and respect licences and maintainers' time.
- Respect privacy. Don't share other people's personal information or private messages without permission.

## Responsible use of skills

Because CDIV teaches security testing, this code has a rule that general open-source codes don't:

- **Never test a system without its owner's written authorization.** That includes systems belonging to other participants, to Fairytale, and to the platforms we use. Practise in your own lab.
- Don't share tools, credentials, data or techniques in CDIV spaces with the intent of harming a real system or person.
- If you find a vulnerability in a real system by accident, report it responsibly to its owner and don't exploit it. For our own systems, see the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, nationality, experience level or appearance.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling and deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Unauthorized testing of any system, or encouraging others to do it.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional learning community.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, tell a maintainer of the [unitCDIV organisation](https://github.com/unitCDIV) privately. If a maintainer is involved, contact a different one. Reports are kept confidential and shared only with the people who need to act on them.

## What happens next

Maintainers review every report and respond as quickly as they can. Depending on what happened, the response may be:

1. **A private conversation**, with an explanation of why the behaviour was a problem.
2. **A formal warning**, with clear consequences if it continues.
3. **A temporary suspension** from some or all CDIV spaces, including cohorts.
4. **A permanent ban**, for serious or repeated violations, and for any unauthorized testing.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code. Anyone subject to an enforcement decision may ask for it to be reviewed by maintainers who were not involved.
