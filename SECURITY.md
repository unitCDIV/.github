# Security policy

Unit 404 (CDIV) teaches security, so we hold our own sites and repositories to the same standard. This policy is the default for every repository in the `unitCDIV` organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through **GitHub's private vulnerability reporting**: [open a draft security advisory](https://github.com/unitCDIV/.github/security/advisories/new) on this repository, or on the affected repository if it has its own. **Don't open a public issue for a security problem.**

Include what you found, where, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We aim to acknowledge reports within five working days.

The curriculum site also publishes a machine-readable contact at https://unitcdiv.fairytale.ai/.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116).

## Reporting content that could endanger readers

If a lesson, lab or tool could lead someone to harm a real system, report it the same way and we'll treat it as a security issue. Examples: a link to a live target, a missing authorization notice, or lab instructions that expose a vulnerable service to a network.

## Scope

In scope:

- The curriculum site at `unitcdiv.fairytale.ai`
- This landing page at `unitcdiv.github.io/.github`
- Public repositories in the `unitCDIV` organisation

Out of scope:

- Deliberately vulnerable applications named in lessons. Report those to their own projects.
- Denial-of-service testing, and testing of GitHub, Cloudflare or other platforms we use. Report those to the platform.
- Social engineering of CDIV members or Fairytale staff.

## Safe harbour

We won't pursue or support legal action against good-faith research that stays within this scope, avoids privacy violations and service disruption, and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before public disclosure.
